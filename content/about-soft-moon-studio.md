Soft Moon Studio is an independent editorial website about calm interiors,
gentle home rituals and soft living.

## What We Publish

Our practical guides focus on cozy lighting, decorative home fragrance,
small-space products and simple routines that can make everyday spaces feel more
intentional. Reflective essays share personal perspectives without presenting
them as universal health advice.

## How Product Guides Are Created

Products are selected for a clear reader need, design fit, practical features
and honest tradeoffs. We review current product listings and specifications
before publication. When a product has not been personally tested, the guide
says so directly.

AI-generated product representations are visibly labelled and are never
presented as official manufacturer or Amazon photography.

## Sources and Fact-Checking

Factual claims are checked against appropriate primary or authoritative sources,
including government health and energy agencies. Relevant sources appear at the
end of the article so readers can verify the information independently.

Wellness content is educational and reflective. It is not medical advice and
does not replace care from a qualified professional.

## Affiliate Transparency

Soft Moon Studio participates in the Amazon Associates programme. The site may
earn a commission from qualifying purchases without changing the price paid by
the reader. Affiliate relationships do not determine whether limitations or
reasons to skip a product are included.

Read the full [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Updates and Corrections

Articles are reviewed when product information, links or source material change.
Meaningful updates are reflected in the article's updated date. Corrections are
made directly rather than silently preserving information known to be outdated.
