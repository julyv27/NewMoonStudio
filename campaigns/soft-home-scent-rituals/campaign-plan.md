# Soft Home Scent Rituals Campaign

## Campaign Purpose

Build a second content pillar for Soft Moon Studio alongside cozy lighting. This
campaign focuses on beautiful home fragrance, essential oil diffusers and gentle
scent rituals, with a balance between commercial buying content and useful
supporting articles.

The campaign should reinforce the broader **Soft Home Rituals** positioning. It
must not make the website look like a catalogue of products or a site focused
only on diffusers.

## Blog Types

### 1. Buying Guide

A commercial article for readers who are actively comparing products. It shows
multiple suitable options, explains the differences and helps the reader choose
for a specific room, need or budget.

Affiliate links are a central and expected part of this article type.

### 2. Product Comparison

A focused comparison of two or three specific products or product categories.
It serves readers who already understand what they want but still need to choose
between premium, affordable or differently featured options.

Do not call this a product review unless Soft Moon Studio has actually tested the
products.

### 3. Practical Problem-Solving Article

An informational article that answers a specific search question or solves a
practical problem. Products may support the solution, but they are not the main
subject.

These articles should naturally link readers to the relevant buying guide.

### 4. Inspiration or Background Article

An editorial article that builds interest, context and trust. It explains an
idea, aesthetic or approach and is generally less commercial.

Its primary role is to attract and inspire readers before directing interested
visitors toward practical or commercial content.

### 5. Ritual Article

A Soft Moon Studio-specific form of inspirational content. It demonstrates how
a product or practice can fit into a gentle morning, evening or home ritual.

Products should feel supportive rather than inserted for the sake of an
affiliate link.

### 6. Product Review

An in-depth assessment of one exact product based on first-hand use. Soft Moon
Studio should publish this type only after genuinely testing the product.

Until then, exact products may be described as options, picks or comparison
participants, but not as personally tested recommendations or reviews.

## Content Funnel

The main route is:

`Pinterest or Google -> supporting article -> diffuser buying guide -> Amazon`

Alternative routes are:

`Pinterest or Google -> buying guide -> Amazon`

`supporting article -> storage buying guide -> Amazon`

The diffuser buying guide is the commercial center of the campaign. Supporting
articles provide reach, context and trust. They should link to the buying guide,
while the buying guide should link back to relevant educational articles where a
reader needs more explanation.

## Internal Linking Rules

- Every supporting article should link to the main diffuser buying guide where
  the reader moves from inspiration to product selection.
- The main buying guide should link to the bedroom guide, scent-routine article
  and evening ritual where they answer a relevant decision question.
- The storage buying guide should link to the main diffuser guide and scent
  routine article.
- Existing articles about feminine wellness, lavender oil, soft mornings and
  evening calm may link into this cluster when the connection is genuinely
  relevant.
- Avoid forcing diffuser links into unrelated lighting articles.

## Commercial Balance

For the first six articles, use this balance:

- 2 buying guides
- 2 practical problem-solving articles
- 1 ritual article
- 1 inspiration or background article

Not every article needs several affiliate cards. Buying guides may contain
multiple product options, while supporting articles should use fewer products
and prioritize the reader's question.

## Editorial Guardrails

- Do not claim that aromatherapy treats anxiety, regulates the nervous system,
  improves sleep or provides another medical outcome.
- Describe scent in terms of atmosphere, personal preference, routine and home
  experience.
- Clearly distinguish verified product specifications from editorial judgment.
- Do not imply first-hand testing when none took place.
- Verify current Amazon products, availability, specifications and destination
  links immediately before publication.
- Use clear affiliate disclosures on commercial articles.
- Prefer products that are visually consistent with Soft Moon Studio but do not
  choose appearance at the expense of safety, usability or honest limitations.

## Conversion Upgrade: June 8, 2026

The cornerstone guide was strengthened after publication with:

- a visible product and specification verification date
- a more useful comparison table with method and capacity
- a decisive short recommendation that reduces choice overload
- four unique product-card images based on the linked products' general forms
- explicit labels stating that these are AI-generated product representations
- product-specific CTA wording focused on checking current listing details

The guide deliberately does not use invented star ratings, first-hand testing
language, artificial scarcity or unsupported popularity claims. Those devices may
increase short-term clicks, but would weaken trust and product-review quality.
