workspace does not contain a Soft Moon Studio Google Ads developer token,

therefore prioritized by relevance, intent and observed platform language, not

## Pinterest Direction

Pinterest Predicts 2026 identifies **Scent Stacking** as an emerging trend and
specifically suggests complementary combinations of sprays, candles and
diffusers for household brands. This supports the planned Soft Home Scent
Rituals cluster, especially future articles about layering fragrance at home.

Pinterest descriptions should use natural combinations of:

- cozy lighting ideas
- warm bedroom lighting
- bedroom lighting without overhead light
- cordless table lamp for small spaces
- calm home aesthetic
- decorative essential oil diffuser
- scent stacking at home
- soft evening ritual

Do not repeat identical keyword strings across every Pin. Match the phrase to
the exact landing-page promise.

The current cozy-lighting and diffuser Pin plans already follow this principle:
they vary between broad discovery, room-specific, problem-led and buying-guide
phrases while keeping the destination article consistent.
