# Cozy Lighting Ideas Pinterest Campaign

## Goal

Drive qualified Pinterest traffic to:

`https://softmoonstudio.com/posts/9-cozy-lighting-ideas-that-make-your-home-feel-softer-at-night/`

The landing page contains nine disclosed Amazon affiliate cards.

## Board Setup

Create the board:

`Cozy Lighting Ideas`

Description:

`Cozy lighting ideas for bedrooms, living rooms and quiet evening routines. Discover warm lamps, renter-friendly wall lights, soft bedside lighting, reading-corner inspiration and simple ways to create a calmer home atmosphere.`

Primary campaign board: `Cozy Lighting Ideas`

Secondary board for selected later variants: `Calm Home Aesthetic`

## Publishing Rhythm

Publish the first ten Pins over five weeks at two Pins per week. Do not bulk-upload them. Keep `pin-11` and `pin-12` in reserve until the first outbound-click data can be reviewed.

## Creative Rationale

- `pin-01`, `pin-06`: broad listicle intent.
- `pin-02`, `pin-11`: problem-led intent.
- `pin-03`: nightstand-lamp and bedroom intent.
- `pin-09`: bedside intent.
- `pin-04`: renter intent.
- `pin-05`, `pin-12`: evening-ritual intent.
- `pin-07`: small-space intent.
- `pin-08`: layering tutorial intent.
- `pin-10`: reading-corner intent.

## Measurement

Review at `21-30` days after the first Pin:

- Pinterest impressions
- saves and save rate
- outbound clicks and outbound click rate
- website sessions per `utm_content`
- affiliate-link clicks from the article
- Amazon orders, commission, and earnings per click

Scale a concept only when it produces qualified outbound traffic. Do not select winners from impressions alone.
