# Soft Home Scent Rituals Pinterest Campaign

## Campaign Goal

Drive qualified Pinterest traffic to the commercial cornerstone:

`https://softmoonstudio.com/posts/best-essential-oil-diffusers-that-look-like-home-decor/`

The landing page compares four exact diffuser options and contains disclosed
Amazon affiliate links.

## Campaign Size

Publish **10 original Pins** over five weeks at two Pins per week.

All ten Pins in this first campaign phase link to the diffuser buying guide. Do
not send scent-specific Pins to the older wellness or lighting articles: those
pages do not match the diffuser-focused promise closely enough.

When the planned supporting articles are live, create separate follow-up Pin
sets for those URLs rather than redirecting these ten concepts.

## Creative Standard

Each Pin should be a fresh **1000 x 1500 px** vertical composition with a clear
mobile-readable headline. Keep the main visual in the middle, the headline in a
clean upper text area and `SOFT MOON STUDIO` small at the bottom.

Use warm ivory, oatmeal, stone, muted taupe, pale oak and restrained eucalyptus
green. Maintain strong text contrast and generous negative space.

Do not:

- reuse the same image with only a different headline
- show a generic image as if it were an exact Amazon product
- copy Amazon product photography
- use medical or emotional-health promises
- cover the full image with text
- add prices, ratings or availability that may change

Exact branded products may appear only when the image source and Amazon usage
method are permitted. Otherwise use clearly generic decorative diffuser scenes.

## Pin Concepts

### Pin 01: Broad Buying Guide

**Headline on Pin:** `4 Essential Oil Diffusers That Look Like Decor`

Use an editorial console scene with three or four visibly different generic
diffuser silhouettes in matte ceramic, glass and wood. The variety should be
understandable immediately. Keep the background warm and uncluttered.

### Pin 02: Problem-Led

**Headline on Pin:** `Diffusers That Don't Look Like Appliances`

Create a split visual: one side hints at a generic shiny plastic appliance shape
in shadow; the brighter main side shows a calm ceramic diffuser integrated into
a styled room. Keep the contrast tasteful rather than mocking.

### Pin 03: Ceramic Design

**Headline on Pin:** `The Best Ceramic Diffusers for a Calm Home`

Show one matte ceramic diffuser as the focal point on pale oak with a linen book
and plaster wall. Use a close editorial crop that emphasizes material and shape.

### Pin 04: Format Comparison

**Headline on Pin:** `Ceramic or Waterless Diffuser?`

Use a clean two-column comparison: a generic ceramic ultrasonic diffuser with a
fine mist on the left and a generic glass-and-wood nebulizer on the right. Add
small labels `ULTRASONIC` and `WATERLESS`, but no technical claims in the image.

### Pin 05: Bedroom Intent

**Headline on Pin:** `Decorative Diffusers for a Calm Bedroom`

Show a diffuser on a spacious nightstand with linen bedding, one closed book and
soft morning or early-evening light. Avoid candles, sleep promises and excessive
mist. Leave clear space above the diffuser.

### Pin 06: Large-Capacity Intent

**Headline on Pin:** `A Large Diffuser Can Still Look Beautiful`

Show a larger generic ceramic diffuser on a living-room console. Use surrounding
objects to communicate scale: one low bowl, a framed neutral print and a single
branch. The diffuser must remain the clear focal point.

### Pin 07: Small-Space Intent

**Headline on Pin:** `Small Ceramic Diffusers for Bedside Tables`

Use a narrow bedside or desk surface with a compact stone-look diffuser, leaving
visible breathing room around it. The scene should communicate a small footprint
without using measurements that have not been verified.

### Pin 08: Buying Checklist

**Headline on Pin:** `7 Things to Check Before Buying a Diffuser`

Create a clean checklist-style Pin over a subtle diffuser background. Include
seven short labels: `METHOD`, `CAPACITY`, `LIGHT`, `TIMER`, `AUTO-OFF`, `CLEANING`,
and `PLACEMENT`. Keep the typography dominant and readable on mobile.

### Pin 09: Display-Worthy Design

**Headline on Pin:** `Diffusers Worth Leaving on Display`

Use an elevated shelf or console still life with one ceramic form and one
glass-and-wood form. The visual should feel like an interior magazine image, not
an Amazon product collage.

### Pin 10: Decision Guide

**Headline on Pin:** `Which Essential Oil Diffuser Fits Your Home?`

Create four simple visual zones or shelves representing: `PREMIUM CERAMIC`,
`LARGE CERAMIC`, `COMPACT`, and `WATERLESS`. Use generic silhouettes and a clear
comparison layout that promises decision help.

## Measurement

Review the first results 21-30 days after Pin 01 and again after all ten Pins
have been live for at least 30 days.

Track:

- impressions
- saves and save rate
- outbound clicks and outbound click rate
- sessions by `utm_content`
- engaged sessions on the buying guide
- affiliate-card clicks by product
- Amazon orders, commission and earnings per click

Do not choose winners from impressions alone. Scale the visual angle that creates
qualified outbound visits and affiliate activity.

## Current Pinterest Guidance

Verified on 2026-06-08 from Pinterest Business guidance:

- Use high-quality vertical creative; Pinterest recommends a 2:3 ratio.
- Publish original content consistently.
- Add a relevant title, description, link and board context.
- Avoid repeatedly uploading duplicate Pins.
- Design for mobile viewing and keep the Pin promise aligned with the landing
  page.
