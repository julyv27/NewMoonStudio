Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Soft Moon Studio is a small editorial site about calm home ideas, soft living, feminine wellness and related inspiration.

This page explains, in plain language, how the site handles privacy when you visit it.

Information We Collect

Soft Moon Studio does not currently offer account registration, comments or a contact form on the site.

That means I do not intentionally collect personal information directly through the website.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process basic server information when you visit, such as:

  • IP address
  • browser type
  • device information
  • page requests
  • approximate location derived from IP

How Your Information May Be Used

Any information processed by the host or by third-party services may be used to:

  • keep the site working
  • understand traffic at a basic level
  • prevent abuse or technical issues
  • show or measure affiliate referrals where applicable

Cookies And Third-Party Services

Soft Moon Studio uses external services and links, including:

  • Amazon affiliate links
  • Pinterest links and embeds
  • Cloudflare hosting
  • third-party CDNs used by the theme

These third parties may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

If I add analytics, forms or other tracking tools later, I will update this page to reflect that.

Affiliate Links

Some pages on Soft Moon Studio contain affiliate links, including Amazon affiliate links. If you click one of those links and make a purchase, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Read the separate Affiliate Disclosure for the short version.

Data Sharing

I do not sell personal information.

I may have limited visibility into basic website activity through hosting or affiliate platforms, depending on what is enabled.

Your Choices

You can:

  • use your browser settings to block or delete cookies
  • avoid clicking third-party links if you prefer not to share referral data
  • leave the site at any time

Changes

I may update this page if the website changes or if I enable new tools in the future.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, contact Soft Moon Studio through the website or the Pinterest profile.
